the rules

$10,000 per portfolio

Each strategy starts with the same amount. No reloads, no extra capital. If it goes to zero, it stays there.

Real closing prices

Every position is marked at its actual market close. No simulated prices, no synthetic data.

AI picks everything

Research, stock selection, position sizing — all done by the AI. Reasoning is documented before each trade. No human overrides.

Open ledger

Every holding, every trade, every dollar is published. You can verify any number against the market yourself.
