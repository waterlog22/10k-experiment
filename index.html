              Method

              $10,000 each

              Starting capital per portfolio. No reloads, no margin. Every strategy gets the same shot, and none of them props up another.

              Measured against VOO

              The benchmark is the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF over the same days the portfolio has been running. “vs S&P” is the portfolio's return minus VOO's.

              Zero overrides

              The AI selects every stock, sizes every position and times every entry and exit. No human steps in.

              Real prices

              Positions are marked to actual closing prices from public markets. Nothing is simulated.

              Started June 18, 2026

              The original portfolios were funded on the same day. Portfolios added later are measured from their own start date.

              Not backtested

              Every trade is made in real time. There is no hindsight, and the AI does not know what prices will do tomorrow.

              What happens each trading day

              1. Fetch closing prices

                After the market closes, the AI pulls the real closing price of every holding in every portfolio.

              2. Check stops and targets

                Each position is checked against its strategy's rules: stop losses, profit targets, rebalancing triggers and income due.

              3. Make the trades

                The AI decides whether to hold, trim, add or exit. The reasoning is written down before anything is executed.

              4. Update the ledger

                Every trade, dividend and price change is recorded, and this site is republished with the new numbers.

              5. Report

                A nightly report covers what happened and why. Strategies that are not working get flagged.

              The strategies

              How decisions get made

              Position sizing

              No single stock is more than 35% of its portfolio at entry. Concentration is allowed when conviction is high, but one name cannot sink the account.

              Entries

              Each strategy has its own signals: valuation screens for the value books, disclosure filings for the political mirrors, sector strength for climate. The reason is recorded before buying.

              Exits

              Short-term books use trailing stops and profit targets. Long-term books act only when a thesis breaks. Every exit follows a rule.

              Income

              Dividends, interest and distributions are tracked separately from price gains and counted in the portfolio's value.

              Benchmark

              Each portfolio is compared with VOO over its own holding period, so a portfolio that started later is not flattered or punished by timing.

              Retirement

              When a strategy is dropped, its portfolio is retired and its final value is frozen on the record. It stops counting in the rankings.

              What this is not

              • Financial advice

                This is a research experiment with fictional portfolios. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.

              • A trading signal

                Sizes, entry prices and timing are specific to this experiment's constraints. Copying these trades in a real account is discouraged.

              • One big fund

                The portfolios are never added together. The point is to see what a single $10,000 would have done under each strategy.
