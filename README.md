# The 10K Experiment

An ongoing paper-trading experiment: $10,000 allocated to each of 15 portfolios across 9 strategy families — day trading, long-term compounding, climate tech, congressional mirrors, income generation, and more.

Every portfolio uses real market prices. No real money is at risk.

## What's inside

- **index.html** — Homepage with scoreboard, strategy grid, and rules
- **dashboard.html** — Analytics dashboard with treemap, allocation rings, and performance bars
- **methodology.html** — Strategy deep-dives, decision framework, and daily process
